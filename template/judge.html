How it works.

  1. 1Each team has four cards: their answers, their site, their demo video, and the rubric. Flip them with the tabs or the arrow keys.
  2. 2On the rubric card, click the sentence that fits best for each criterion, from 1 (poor) to 5 (outstanding), and add a note for the deliberation.
  3. 3Press Save, or Next team to save and move on. Previous and Next at the top also move between teams, and keep your marks.
